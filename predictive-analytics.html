Signature capability 02

Predictive analytics should prevent losses, not just predict them

I design early-warning and decision analytics that identify risk and opportunity in time to act — and connect them to frontline action, capacity and measurement so the value is real.

The executive problem

Why organizations struggle here

Lagging indicators

Attrition is measured at account closure, months after the relationship began to weaken.

Models without workflows

Risk scores sit in notebooks; branches, contact centers and marketers can’t use them.

Unproven value

Results are reported as “customers contacted” rather than incremental saves against a control.

My approach

Make the signal actionable

The model is the easy part. Value comes from routing the right response to the right team — and proving it changed behavior.
