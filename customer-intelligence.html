Signature capability 01

Customer intelligence only matters when it changes decisions

I build enterprise customer intelligence that joins feedback, behavior, journeys and research — and connects it to the owners, KPIs and decisions that change customer and business outcomes.

The executive problem

Why organizations struggle here

Feedback without ownership

Organizations collect millions of comments and scores, but recurring pain points have no accountable owner outside the insights team.

Segments that sit on a shelf

Persona libraries and segmentations are built once, then ignored by Product, Marketing and Operations.

Insight teams as report factories

Analysts spend their time on requests and reconciliation instead of advising leaders on what to do next.

My approach

Join the signals, govern the action

I design the full system: the listening architecture, the analytics that explain drivers and value, and the governance that makes sure someone owns each fix.
