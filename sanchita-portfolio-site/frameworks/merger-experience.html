Enterprise Framework 04

When Two Brands Become One: Protecting Trust, Loyalty and Relationship Value

Built for the Space City Credit Union integration at TDECU: a framework connecting brand transition, member listening, journey friction, relationship behavior and retention — so leaders see merger risk before it shows up as lost relationships.

Framework I designedPilotedIllustrative visual, not employer data

Where this comes from

Designed for the TDECU–Space City Credit Union integration

Mergers are usually measured on systems, costs and timelines. I designed a framework to measure what customers experience: whether they understand the change, trust the combined organization, and keep their relationship. At TDECU, I built this approach for the Space City Credit Union integration.

What the framework covers

  • Pre-merger baseline of brand awareness, perception and trust
  • Transition listening: communication effectiveness, confusion and sentiment
  • Conversion friction across digital, branch and contact center journeys
  • Post-merger NPS, ease, intent to stay and actual retention
  • Behavioral early-warning signals and relationship strength
  • Executive merger scorecard

Status

  • Framework I designed: phases, listening ecosystem, scorecard and risk index structure
  • Piloted: tiered White Glove engagement for 491 high-value members
  • Result: more than $2.8M retained through escheatment intervention
  • Proposed methodology: the composite Brand Transition Risk Index; not a validated or deployed production score
  • No member survey results are shown
