Signature capability 03

Market intelligence is how leaders decide where to play and how to win

I connect household, competitive and geographic intelligence with customer needs to guide segment choices, product investment, acquisition and retention — and measure whether those choices paid off.

The executive problem

Why organizations struggle here

Growth plans without evidence

Targets are set from last year’s results rather than from market opportunity and competitive position.

Disconnected functions

Strategy, Product and Marketing each use different segments and different numbers.

Campaigns measured on volume

Conversion is celebrated without knowing whether it was profitable or incremental.

My approach

Choose with evidence, then prove it

I build the intelligence that informs the choice, connect Strategy, Product and Marketing around it, and link results back to the segment.
