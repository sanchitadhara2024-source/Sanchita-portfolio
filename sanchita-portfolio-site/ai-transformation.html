Signature capability 04

AI transformation starts with trusted intelligence and clear decisions

I develop enterprise roadmaps that move organizations from descriptive reporting toward predictive intelligence, AI-assisted insight, experimentation and next-best-action decisions — governed, measured and honest about what is deployed versus explored.

The executive problem

Why organizations struggle here

Tools before foundations

AI pilots launch on top of untrusted data and inconsistent KPIs, then stall.

No decision owner

Use cases are chosen for novelty rather than for a decision someone is accountable for.

Overstated maturity

Proofs of concept are presented as production capability, eroding executive trust.

My approach

Climb the ladder in order

Each step depends on the one below it. I build the data, KPI and governance foundations first, then add predictive and AI capabilities where action owners are ready.
