Enterprise Framework 03

Connecting Intelligence to Enterprise Growth Decisions

An operating model that links where an organization chooses to compete, what it builds and how it goes to market — with shared intelligence closing the loop.

Framework I designedIllustrative visual, not employer data

The idea

One decision architecture, three roles

“Strategy defines where to play and how to win. Product translates those choices into customer value. Marketing activates that value in the market. Intelligence and analytics continuously inform the next decision.”

The problem it solves

  • Roadmaps that don’t reflect strategic segments
  • Campaigns promoting products that don’t fit the audience
  • Growth targets no one can trace to a specific choice
  • Analytics teams producing different numbers for the same question

Where it draws from

  • At Walmart, research on willingness to pay, features and shipping value laid foundations for free shipping and future Walmart+; the free-shipping loyalty launch lifted online sales 45% in its first quarter
  • At TDECU, common segmentation, RACI and governance connected Strategy, Product and Marketing; segment-level campaigns delivered 4× CD conversion and $4.53M in mortgage funding
  • The full operating model on this page is a framework I designed
