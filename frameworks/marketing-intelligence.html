Enterprise Framework 02

From Disconnected Reporting to Predictive Marketing Intelligence

How I design an enterprise marketing intelligence function — data, research, segmentation, measurement and activation — that moves beyond reporting to influence strategic investment, product and customer decisions.

Implemented in prior rolesFramework I designedFuture-state conceptIllustrative visual, not employer data

Where this comes from

Built from enterprise marketing analytics work at TDECU, Walmart and Bloomingdale’s

At TDECU I designed the Marketing Intelligence maturity and operating model and led segmentation, campaign measurement and attribution. Earlier work at Walmart and Bloomingdale’s on segmentation, loyalty and research shaped the same playbook.

Implemented in prior roles

  • Standardized KPIs: campaign ROI, lift, conversion, product penetration and lifetime value as a single source of truth
  • Segmentation and personalization (Claritas P$YCLE, generational, geographic, lifecycle)
  • Predictive at-risk churn, propensity, cross-sell and product-fit models connected to marketing, branches and contact centers
  • Segment-level campaign research and attribution: 4× CD conversion (+1,368 wins); 215 mortgage applications and $4.53M funded

Designed or future-state

  • The Reactive → Proactive → Predictive journey, five-level maturity curve and eight-step roadmap (framework design)
  • MMM / attribution, experimentation and AI readiness (operating model design)
  • Governed GenAI research synthesis, journey simulation and next-best-action (explored / future-state, not presented as deployed)

Visual 1

Three operating states: Reactive → Proactive → Predictive

The real test of maturity is whether insight changes the next decision — which segment to pursue, which proposition to build, which intervention to launch and where to invest.

Visual 2

A five-level capability maturity curve

The journey from reactive to predictive is supported by a five-level capability curve, so progress can be planned and measured step by step.

Visual 3

Six capability pillars assessed at each stage

Organizations are rarely at one level everywhere. Rating each pillar separately keeps a team from being called predictive just because one group built a model.

Visual 4

The eight-step implementation roadmap

How I move a marketing analytics function from reporting to strategic decisioning.

Visual 5

The executive scorecard: measure the decision chain

Pair operating metrics with customer outcomes and commercial economics. When results miss plan, the chain shows whether the issue was the choice, the offer, the activation or the measurement.

Visual 6

Conceptual executive marketing intelligence dashboard

A layout I use to focus leadership reviews on growth, efficiency and retention. Trend lines are placeholders, not results.
