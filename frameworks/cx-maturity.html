Enterprise Framework 01

From Reactive Customer Feedback to Proactive Enterprise CX Intelligence

How I design the operating model behind customer experience — vision, governance, listening, journeys and closed-loop accountability — so feedback turns into decisions and measurable results.

Implemented in prior rolesFramework I designedIllustrative visual, not employer data

Where this comes from

Designed and applied at CVS Health and TDECU

I have built CX capabilities in organizations at very different stages: scaling an enterprise Voice of Customer program at CVS Health, and building a CX capability roadmap, charter and governance model at TDECU. This framework distills what worked in both.

The challenge

  • Feedback collected at scale but not consistently driving decisions
  • Recurring pain points with no accountable owner outside the CX team
  • Scores reported without a clear link to retention or revenue
  • Frontline teams hearing about feedback weeks after the moment

What I built

  • Enterprise CX vision, charter and strategic priorities
  • Cross-functional governance and RACI across Marketing, Product, Digital, Operations and Technology
  • Voice of Customer / Member measurement architecture (NPS, CSAT/MSAT, closed loop)
  • Five new journey maps, including onboarding, deposits and digital
  • An action forum linking feedback to owners, deadlines and measures
  • Executive and Board-level CX reporting

The five foundations of enterprise CX transformation

Visual 2

The operating cycle

The discipline is not in any one step; it is in keeping the cycle moving every month, with governance holding each stage accountable.

Visual 3

The cross-functional CX operating model

CX teams cannot fix experiences alone. The model gives the CX Center of Excellence a clear role and puts accountability for fixes with the business owners who control them.

Visual 4

Decision rights: an interactive RACI

Use the buttons to see who is accountable, who does the work and who is consulted for each recurring CX decision.

Visual 5

Stage 1 diagnostic: the capability heatmap

The Assess & Equip stage rates seven capabilities. Markers show a common starting point and the target; it identifies the few gaps that block progress rather than recommending a wholesale platform replacement.

Visual 6

Executive CX scorecard

Three tiers connect experience to the economics leaders care about. Values are left out deliberately; the structure is the point.
